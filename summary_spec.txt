SYSTEM ARCHITECTURAL GOVERNING PRINCIPLE
All safety-critical thermal monitoring, multi-rail electrical supervision, forward temperature trend projection, and 
protective shutdown decisions execute autonomously within the local ESP32 firmware loop. The ATX switch-mode power 
supply (SMPS) remains fully monitored and controllable locally from uninterrupted +5VSB standby power, even during 
total external internet, Wi-Fi, or cloud service outages.
PROJECT PLATFORM & SCOPE DELIMITATION
This specification establishes the hardware, firmware, and supervisory implementation for demonstrating condition 
monitoring and predictive adaptive thermal control on a small-scale ATX switch-mode power supply assembly. This 
design demonstrates the monitoring and control principles using low-voltage instrumentation – it does not constitute a 
certified utility-grid transformer protective device, substation protection relay, or safety-instrumented emergency-stop 
(SIS/E-stop) system.

SCOPE BOUNDARY & DEMONSTRATION OBJECTIVE
The objective of this project is to demonstrate condition-monitoring, predictive thermal-control, and supervisory-
protection principles using a small-scale ATX switch-mode power supply (SMPS) bench. It is not intended to construct 
an industrial grid transformer-protection system or replace certified primary overcurrent devices.
1.1 Interpretation
• “Shall” identifies a mandatory implementation requirement.
• “Proposed” identifies an engineering value, threshold, or method that requires experimental bench testing before 
final commissioning acceptance.
2. Abstract
The project monitors the surface temperature of an SMPS transformer, inlet-air temperature, and the voltage, current, 
and power of three DC output rails: 12 V, 5 V, and 3.3 V.
An ESP32 microcontroller processes all sensor measurements locally, predicts near-term temperature rise through 
numerical trend extrapolation, and modulates a two-wire fan through a Toshiba TB6612FNG dual-bridge driver. The 
controller requests emergency supervisory shutdown of all main outputs via the ATX PS_ON# active-low interface 
when validated thermal or electrical trip conditions occur.
The ESP32 operates continuously from the SMPS +5VSB standby supply so that condition monitoring, event logging, 
and telemetry reporting continue uninterrupted even after main outputs are disabled. Selected thermal trip incidents 
may permit a qualified automatic restart following verified cooldown, whereas electrical overcurrent and bus fault trips 
remain latched permanently for physical inspection.
The system provides:
• An offline, zero-dependency dashboard hosted directly by the ESP32 via LittleFS.
• An internet-accessible real-time dashboard hosted on Firebase Hosting.
• Persistent time-series history, statistical summaries, and fault event logs stored in Firebase Realtime Database.
• Transactional email alert notifications dispatched via a Cloudflare Worker invoking the Brevo API.
• Independent heartbeat communication-loss supervision executed by scheduled Cloudflare Cron Triggers.
• Explainable, condition-based maintenance advisories derived from thermal trends and fault history without unsup­
ported component failure-life claims.

3.1 Exclusions
The initial implementation explicitly does not provide:
• Direct measurement of internal transformer winding hot-spot temperature, core magnetic flux, or insulation dielectric 
degradation.
• Insulation-health remaining useful life (RUL) prognostic estimation.
• AC mains line input-power or power-factor measurement.
• Actual fan tachometer (RPM) pulse feedback or blade stall detection.
• Individual branch load disconnection (all main outputs are interrupted together via PS_ON#).
• Primary certified circuit-breaker or fast short-circuit protection (the INA219 is an instrument, not a circuit breaker).
• Safety-instrumented system (SIS) or emergency-stop (E-stop) functional safety certification.

7.2 Switched Domain
Main 12 V, 5 V, and 3.3 V rails are enabled or disabled simultaneously via the PS_ON# line. When disabled:
• External loads (LED strip and USB converter) immediately lose power.
• The 12 V cooling fan ceases operation due to absence of motor supply.
• The ESP32 controller, sensor instrumentation, and LittleFS web server remain active on standby power.
• System cooldown occurs via passive natural convection.
• Real-time diagnostic reporting and telemetry upload continue uninterrupted.
7.3 Power Requirements
1. Verify manufacturer nameplate confirms +5VSB is rated for continuous current ≥1.5 A.
2. Feed power strictly into the ESP32 5 V pin; never feed 5 V directly to the 3.3 V rail.
3. Under no circumstances join the +5VSB standby rail to the main switched 5 V rail.
4. Prevent USB programming cable backfeeding using Schottky diode or MOSFET isolation.
5. Verify brownout immunity during Wi-Fi transmission power spikes (≈240 mA).

10. Predictive Thermal-Management Algorithm
10.1 Acquisition
• Proposed temperature sampling interval: Δ𝑡= 1.0 second.
• DS18B20 12-bit conversion is initiated asynchronously (750 ms max). The scratchpad register is read only after 
conversion completion.
• Each sample record contains the float temperature value, validity boolean, monotonic timestamp, and CRC error 
status.
• Corrupted or invalid samples are discarded and never enter the trend estimation buffer.
10.2 Digital Filtering
Raw sensor noise is filtered using an Exponential Moving Average (EMA):

• Proposed baseline filter coefficient: 𝛼= 0.2.
• The filter is initialized directly from the first verified valid measurement.
• Protection Invariant: Filtered temperature 𝑇𝑓 is used strictly for trend projection and fan demand modulation. 
Unfiltered raw readings 𝑇(𝑘) are supervised concurrently by the protection loop to guarantee zero trip latency 
during sudden thermal excursions.

winding hot spots, core flux saturation, or thermal insulation aging.

12.2 Qualified Thermal Automatic Recovery Rules
Automatic re-energization after a thermal shutdown is strictly conditional and permitted only when:
1. Connected loads are verified safe for unattended re-energization.
2. The initial trip was caused exclusively by a recoverable thermal event (not electrical overcurrent).
3. All temperature and rail sensors report valid, fresh measurements.
4. Transformer surface temperature has dropped below a separate restart threshold: 𝑇≤𝑇restart.
5. Measured temperature is stable or decreasing over a verified observation period (≥300 seconds).
6. Minimum cooling dwell time (≥600 seconds) has fully elapsed.
7. No manual OFF switch assertion or remote emergency stop inhibit is active.
8. Remaining automatic retry allowance is available (𝑁retry > 0). Default policy: 1 retry per incident.
12.3 Mandatory Restart Restrictions
• Electrical trips never restart automatically. Manual bench inspection is mandatory.
• An authorized remote OFF command immediately cancels any pending automatic thermal recovery.
• Network reconnection or cloud sync events never trigger an automatic startup sequence.
• A maintained physical ON switch position does not bypass fault lockouts.
• Controller MCU resets always default main outputs to disabled (STANDBY_OFF).

17. Buffering and Historical Retention
17.1 Rolling Circular RAM Buffer
The ESP32 firmware dedicates a 32 KB static heap allocation to an in-memory circular ring buffer holding up to 120 
minutes of 60-second summary telemetry frames (120 records):
• Each record packs timestamp (4 bytes), 1-minute averaged temperatures (8 bytes), peak temperatures (4 bytes), 3-
rail electrical means (12 bytes), and system status bitflags (2 bytes).
• Total record footprint: 30 bytes per minute (3.6 KB per 120-minute retention cycle).
• During active Wi-Fi or cloud outages, telemetry records accumulate sequentially without packet loss.
• If an outage exceeds 120 minutes, the oldest records are dropped using a First-In-First-Out (FIFO) queue policy, 
preserving uninterrupted operational safety without risking heap exhaustion.
17.2 Network Backfill and Exponential Backoff
Upon verified Wi-Fi reconnection and Firebase token re-authorization:
1. The ESP32 enters BACKFILL_DRAIN mode on background Core 0.
2. Buffered records are dispatched in chronological batches of 5 records per HTTPS POST to /history.
3. An exponential backoff retry policy (1 s →2 s →4 s →8 s →16 s) handles transient HTTP 429 (Rate Limit) or 503 
(Service Unavailable) responses.
4. Live real-time streaming to /latest takes immediate precedence over backfill uploads to ensure operator visibility 
of current machine status.
17.3 Cloud Storage Pruning Lifecycle
To operate continuously within the Firebase Spark free-tier limit (1 GB total storage, 10
GB
month outbound transfer):
• Telemetry at /latest is overwritten in place (zero incremental storage growth).
• 1-minute historical frames at /history are retained for a rolling 30-day window.
• A Cloudflare Worker scheduled task executes weekly at 00:00 UTC to prune records older than 30 days via Firebase 
REST API authentication.

19. Security and Privacy
1. Zero Plaintext Transmission: All external communications between ESP32, Firebase, Cloudflare, and browser clients 
utilize mandatory TLS 1.3 transport encryption.
2. Database Access Rules: Firebase Realtime Database Security Rules enforce granular read/write authorization. 
Public anonymous clients possess read-only rights to /latest and /history; write operations to /latest and /events 
require pre-shared ESP32 database secret tokens.
3. Remote Command Authorization: The /commands node requires administrative authentication. Remote OFF 
instructions must include a SHA-256 HMAC signature verified by the ESP32 before execution.
4. Strict Time-To-Live (TTL): All remote control tokens carry a 30-second TTL. Stale or delayed commands received 
after expiry are rejected and logged as security anomalies.
5. Uni-Directional Remote Control Boundary: The remote interface supports only supervisory shutdown (Remote 
OFF). Under no circumstances is remote re-energization (Remote ON) permitted over the internet.
6. Credentials Isolation: Wi-Fi SSID, WPA2 passphrases, and Firebase API keys are stored in encrypted ESP32 Non-
Volatile Storage (NVS) partitions and are never exposed via HTTP responses.
7. Local AP Security: The commissioning SoftAP defaults to WPA2-PSK encryption with a unique factory PIN printed 
on the controller enclosure. SoftAP radio is automatically disabled 10 minutes post-boot during normal operation.
8. Data Minimization: Telemetry records contain strictly physical engineering metrics (volts, amperes, degrees Celsius, 
duty cycle). No Personally Identifiable Information (PII), MAC addresses, or location data are stored.
9. CORS & Content Security: Firebase Hosting web dashboard serves strict Content-Security-Policy (CSP) headers 
disallowing unauthorized external scripts or telemetry exfiltration.
10. Tamper Audit Logging: All local configuration updates, manual switch overrides, and failed authentication 
attempts are logged persistently to the /events audit trail.

21. Future Independent Branch / Rail Switching
21.1 Architectural Scope Delimitation
In the baseline Version 3.0 implementation, supervisory shutdown acts globally via the ATX PS_ON# line, which disables 
all switched DC outputs (12 V, 5 V, 3.3 V) simultaneously. While effective, a localized overcurrent event on a single 
branch (such as an LED strip short) currently interrupts all operational rails.
21.2 Proposed Solid-State eFuse Architecture
For post-baseline evolution, an external multi-channel solid-state switching module utilizing high-side P-channel 
MOSFETs or integrated intelligent eFuse ICs (e.g., Texas Instruments TPS2594x) is proposed.

23. Safety Precautions and Commissioning Checklist
MANDATORY ELECTRICAL SAFETY DIRECTIVE
High-voltage alternating current (115 V / 230 V AC) is present within the interior enclosure of the ATX power supply. 
The SMPS protective chassis shall remain completely sealed throughout all installation and operational testing. All 
experimental instrumentation, shunts, sensors, and actuators shall interface strictly to the exposed low-voltage DC 
breakout terminals (<= 12 V DC).
23.1 Pre-Energization Commissioning Checklist
Before connecting the ATX power supply AC line cord to utility mains power, the commissioning engineer must execute 
and verify each sequential gate:
1. Chassis Ground Continuity: Verify low-resistance path (𝑅< 0.2Ω) between SMPS external metal casing and AC 
utility earth pin.
2. Polarity Verification: Confirm positive infeed of all three INA219 channels connects to the intended voltage source 
and VIN- connects to the load side.
3. I2C Bus Addressing Verification: Verify distinct hardware solder jumpers: INA219 1 (0x40), INA219 2 (0x41), INA219 
3 (0x44), LCD (0x27 or 0x3F).
4. DS18B20 Probe Binding: Ensure transformer surface probe (CH1) and ambient inlet probe (CH2) are properly 
registered by their respective 64-bit ROM IDs.
5. Single-Point Star Ground: Verify that all logic grounds, driver power grounds, and sensor grounds converge at a 
single common reference terminal.
6. Inline Protection Fusing: Verify that fast-acting fuses are installed in series with the +5VSB line (1.0 A) and the 12 
V load branch (3.15 A).
